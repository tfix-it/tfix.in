## Startup status and tax

| Fact | Source | Checked |
|---|---|---|
| DPIIT startup recognition: Pvt Ltd, registered partnership, LLP or cooperative society (sole proprietorship not eligible); up to 10 years from incorporation; turnover not above **₹200 crore** in any year (deep-tech category: 20 years, ₹300 crore); innovation or scalable model; not formed by splitting/reconstruction. Revised by G.S.R. 108(E), 4 Feb 2026. Applied via NSWS. | startupindia.gov.in recognition page; PIB 5 Feb 2026; DPIIT site | 25 Nov 2026 |
| **Angel tax abolished**: section 56(2)(viib) not applicable from AY 2025-26 (1 Apr 2025); no equivalent in the Income-tax Act 2025. Source-of-funds scrutiny for share money still applies (section 102(3) of the 2025 Act). | incometaxindia.gov.in; Startup India Tax Playbook May 2026 | 25 Nov 2026 |
| Startup tax holiday: section 80-IAC is now **section 140 of the Income-tax Act 2025** (from 1 Apr 2026): 100% of profits for 3 consecutive years out of 10; company or LLP incorporated 1 Apr 2016–31 Mar 2030; needs **Inter-Ministerial Board certificate** (DPIIT recognition alone is not enough); not available under the 22% concessional regime. ⚠ Turnover cap reported as ₹100 crore by some sources and raised to ₹300 crore by Finance Act 2026 by others — verify on the Act text. | Startup India Tax Playbook; CA commentaries Sep–Nov 2026 | 25 Nov 2026 |
| GST registration in Kerala: aggregate turnover above **₹20 lakh** if any services are supplied (₹40 lakh only for exclusive supply of goods); compulsory from the first rupee in section 24 cases (e.g. inter-state supply of goods, selling via an e-commerce operator, reverse charge). State code 32. | CGST Act s.22; Notification 10/2019-CT | 25 Nov 2026 |

## Funding schemes

| Scheme | Key terms | Source | Checked |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Startup India Seed Fund Scheme (SISFS)** | Via selected incubators. Up to **₹20 lakh grant** (PoC/prototype/trials, milestone tranches) and up to **₹50 lakh** debt/convertible (market entry/scale; at most repo rate, up to 60 months, unsecured). Eligibility: DPIIT-recognised, incorporated ≤ 2 years at application, tech in core, ≥51% Indian promoters, not more than ₹10 lakh from other central/state schemes. Incubators may not charge selection fees. | seedfund.startupindia.gov.in FAQ; DPIIT; Startup India Playbook Jun 2026 | 25 Nov 2026 |
| **KSUM Idea Grant** | Up to **₹3 lakh** for startups (ideation/PoC/MVP; ₹2 lakh student track via IEDC Idea Fest); company incorporation and KSUM Unique ID needed before disbursal; Kerala-based; reapply after 3 months if rejected. | startupmission.kerala.gov.in/schemes/idea-grant | 25 Nov 2026 |
| KSUM Productisation / Market Acceleration / Scale-up grants | Productisation up to ₹7 lakh (if no Idea Grant); Market Acceleration up to ₹10 lakh; Scale-up up to ₹15 lakh; women/transgender productisation up to ₹12 lakh. ⚠ Amounts differ between KSUM documents — verify the current call. | KSUM K-Swift document Feb 2025; KSUM entrepreneurs PDF Mar 2025 | 25 Nov 2026 |
| KSUM other | Seed loan up to ₹15 lakh; marketing support (50% subsidy on marketing expense, per state policy page); Investor Café (last Wednesday monthly, Kochi); Fund of Funds invests as LP in SEBI AIFs (16 funds partnered) that back Kerala startups. | KSUM pages; Startup India Kerala policy page | 25 Nov 2026 |
| **PM MUDRA Yojana** | Collateral-free loans: Shishu ≤ ₹50,000; Kishor ₹50,000–5 lakh; Tarun ₹5–10 lakh; **Tarun Plus ₹10–20 lakh** (only after repaying a Tarun loan; from 24 Oct 2024). Via banks, NBFCs, MFIs; interest per lender. | financialservices.gov.in; PIB Apr 2026 | 25 Nov 2026 |
| **CGTMSE** | Guarantee for MSE loans without collateral up to **₹10 crore** (from 1 Apr 2025); coverage 75–90% depending on category and size (micro up to ₹5 lakh: 85%; women entrepreneurs 90%). RBI: no collateral for MSE loans up to ₹20 lakh (Master Direction 9 Feb 2026). | dcmsme.gov.in; CGTMSE scheme document Apr 2026; PIB Jul 2026 | 25 Nov 2026 |
| Credit Guarantee Scheme for Startups (CGSS) | Guarantees on debt to DPIIT startups via eligible lenders — check current limits. ⚠ | DPIIT page | 25 Nov 2026 |
| GeM Startup Runway | Sell to government buyers; exemptions from prior experience, turnover and EMD for DPIIT startups. | Startup India Playbook Jun 2026 | 25 Nov 2026 |
